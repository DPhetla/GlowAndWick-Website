Our Story

Crafting Comfort, One Flame at a Time

Welcome to Glow & Wick. Our journey began with a simple passion for creating ambient spaces that speak to the heart. Every single candle we offer is thoughtfully designed and handcrafted right here in Mbombela using premium wax molds and high-quality materials.

We believe that a candle is more than just a source of light-it is a vessel for comfort, memories, and serenity. Thank you for bringing our craftsmanship into your beautiful home and letting our flame illuminate your moments.
